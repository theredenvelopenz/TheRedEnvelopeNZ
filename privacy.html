Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The Red Envelope NZ (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

We are a New Zealand business offering IT services, website building, NFC cards and analytics, an AI receptionist, and marketing services. Our Privacy Officer can be contacted at theredenvelopenz@gmail.com (please put “Privacy” in the subject line).

2. What information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you use the contact form or email us, we collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and anything else you choose to tell us in your message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so that the website can be delivered securely and protected from abuse. We do not use this to identify you or to build a profile about you.
  • Chat assistant. “Scrappy Coco” is an automated assistant, not a person. What you type to Scrappy Coco is processed in your browser to generate a reply. It is not sent to us and we do not store it.

Please do not include sensitive information, such as health details or bank account numbers, in your message. We do not need it to answer an enquiry.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and this website does not load third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the website from bots and abuse. We do not store anything else in your browser.

4. Why we collect your information and how we use it

We collect personal information only when it is necessary for what we do, and we collect it directly from you wherever we can. We use it to:

  • reply to your enquiry and give you a quote;
  • provide, support and invoice the services you ask us for, and keep the records that go with them;
  • keep this website secure and working properly; and
  • meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not send you marketing messages unless you have asked for them or agreed to receive them.

Sometimes we may receive your details from someone else, for example a client who names you as the contact at a site where we are working. If that happens, we will tell you as soon as reasonably practicable that we hold your information and why.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with people and services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website and provides DNS and security services;
  • our email provider (Google, for Gmail), which delivers the messages you send us;
  • a form-delivery service, if we have enabled one, which passes your contact form message to us; and
  • our professional advisers, such as accountants, where needed.

We may also disclose information when the law requires or allows it, for example to the Police or a regulator, or to protect our legal rights.

Some of these providers store or process information outside New Zealand, including in the United States. They handle it on our behalf and we remain responsible for protecting it.

6. Security and how long we keep your information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. This website is served over HTTPS and we limit access to our email and hosting accounts to the people who need it.

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Information about clients and the work we do for them is kept for as long as we need it to provide our services and as the law requires (for example, tax record-keeping rules, which generally require records to be kept for seven years).

7. Privacy breaches

If a privacy breach involving your information is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as soon as practicable, as the Privacy Act 2020 requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we hold personal information about you, to give you access to it, and to correct it if it is wrong. Email our Privacy Officer at the address above. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable and within 20 working days.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner: www.privacy.org.nz, phone 0800 803 909, email enquiries@privacy.org.nz.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
